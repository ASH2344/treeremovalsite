What Is Forestry Mulching?

Forestry mulching uses a machine that cuts and grinds trees and brush into mulch in one step. The mulch stays on the ground, which helps control erosion and saves the cost of hauling debris away. It works well for overgrown lots, trails, and fence lines.

Our Land Clearing Process

  1. Site visit. We walk the property, mark what stays and what goes, and note slopes, access, and nearby structures.
  2. Clear quote. You get a price based on acreage, density, and debris handling.
  3. Clearing. Trees are cut, brush is removed or mulched, and stumps are handled as agreed.
  4. Debris handling. Haul-off, on-site mulching, or a mix, depending on your plans.
  5. Final walk-through. We confirm the site is ready for your next step.

How Much Does Land Clearing Cost in Macon?

Land clearing is priced by the job, not per tree. Cost depends on lot size, how dense the trees and brush are, tree size, slope, whether stumps are removed, and whether debris is hauled or mulched. Call (774) 446-3349 for a free site quote.

Land Clearing FAQs

Do I need a permit to clear land in Macon?

Larger clearing jobs and building sites may need permits or approvals from Macon-Bibb County. Check before work starts, and we'll flag anything we notice.

Can you keep certain trees?

Yes. Selective clearing lets you keep healthy shade trees while removing brush and weak trees.

What happens to the debris?

We can haul it away, mulch it on site, or combine both.

Do you clear lots for new construction?

Yes. We clear building pads, driveways, and access paths.
