Why Trim Your Trees?

  • Protect your home. Branches over roofs scrape shingles, clog gutters, and can break off in storms.
  • Reduce storm damage. Thinned crowns let wind pass through instead of catching like a sail.
  • Keep trees healthy. Removing dead and diseased limbs stops decay from spreading.
  • Clear driveways, sidewalks, and lines. Low limbs get raised so cars and people pass safely.
  • Let in more light. Better sunlight for grass and gardens.

When to Trim Trees in Macon

Late fall through winter is usually the best time for most trees in Middle Georgia. Trees are dormant, branches are easier to see, and there's less stress on the tree.

When to trim pine trees: Late winter to early spring is generally best, before new growth starts. Avoid heavy pine pruning in hot summer months, when fresh cuts can attract pine beetles.

Dead or dangerous limbs can be removed any time of year. Don't wait if a limb is hanging over your house.

Trimming vs. Removal

Trimming is often enough when a tree is healthy but overgrown. Removal makes more sense when a tree is dead, badly decayed, or leaning toward your home. Not sure which you need? Tree Trimming FAQs

How often should trees be trimmed?

Most mature trees benefit from trimming every 3 to 5 years. Younger trees may need it more often while they're developing shape.

Can you trim near power lines?

We can trim branches near the service line to your home. Branches touching main utility lines are usually handled by Georgia Power or your local utility.

Will trimming hurt my tree?

Proper pruning helps trees. Over-trimming or "topping" damages them, so we avoid it.

Do you clean up afterward?

Yes. Branches are hauled off or chipped, and the yard is raked.
