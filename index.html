Trees We Work On Most in Macon

Middle Georgia yards are full of tall loblolly pines, water oaks, live oaks, sweetgums, and old pecans. Water oaks often rot from the inside and drop heavy limbs without warning. Tall pines can snap or uproot in summer thunderstorms. If a tree near your home is leaning, cracked, or losing bark, get it checked before the next storm.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does tree service cost in Macon?

Most tree removals in the Macon area cost roughly $250 to $1,000. Large trees, trees near houses or power lines, and emergency jobs cost more. Trimming usually costs less than removal.

Do I need a permit to remove a tree in Macon?

Usually not for a tree in your own yard. But if your property is in one of Macon's Design Review Districts (zoned historic or downtown), you need a Certificate of Appropriateness first, even for a dead tree. Trees in the public right-of-way need county approval. See our Macon tree removal permit guide.

Can you remove a tree that fell on my house?

Yes. Call (774) 446-3349 right away. Stay clear of the area, especially if power lines are involved.

Do you grind stumps?

Yes. Stump grinding can be added to any tree removal or booked on its own.
